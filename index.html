How it works

1. Share

Plug the device into an Android phone (via OTG) or a Windows PC running USB/IP, and switch on Share.

2. Find

The Windows app finds sharing phones and PCs on your network automatically, or you add one by address.

3. Attach

Click Attach. The device appears in Windows like it's plugged in locally. Tick Auto to reconnect by itself.
